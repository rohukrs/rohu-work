This is a sample essay. It is marked as a **draft**, so it will not appear on the production website until you change `draft: true` to `draft: false`.

I like the idea of having one place for things that do not always fit neatly into a category. Notes from building products, stories from making films, things I am learning, and thoughts I have not quite figured out yet.

A paragraph can be short. It can be one sentence. The page gives each thought enough room to breathe.

You can write in *italics*, use **bold** when something needs emphasis, or add a [link to something interesting](https://example.com).

> A good story does not always need a big conclusion. Sometimes it just needs to be told.

You can also add an image:

`![A short description of the image](/images/your-image.jpg)`

When the essay is ready, change the draft value in the frontmatter and commit the file to GitHub.
